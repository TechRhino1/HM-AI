## Real-spread wiring — investigation complete, decision still gated

`reports/gp11_real_spread_investigation.md`. The real spread is available **twice and is
discarded both times**: `mt5_client.py:563` computes it at order-send only, and
`data_feed.py:378` projects the `spread` column away. 21 downstream consumers. Critically:

* `spread_ratio ≡ 1` today (every live caller passes the registry constant), so every
  spread gate is structurally unable to fire.
* The parquet `spread` column is MT5 **points** → pips = `spread * point / pip_size`.
  `spread * pip_size` double-counts.
* **The naive fix is dangerous**: `position_monitor.py:290` freezes position management at
  `spread > typical*2`, and the real spread exceeds that on **100% of M1 bars** for
  EURUSD/USDJPY/AUDUSD. Feeding the real spread in wholesale would kill trailing stops,
  breakeven and partial closes on exactly those majors.
* The `ask > 0 and bid > 0` fallback at `dynamic_levels.py:139` is **unreachable** in live.

Recommended order (not yet actioned): Step 0 keep the column (zero behaviour change) →
Step 1 new `MarketContext.live_spread_pips` for reporting + EV cost only, leaving
`vol.current_spread_pips` on the registry value → Step 2 geometry after a backtest A/B →
Step 3 gates last, and fix the blowout guard before any of it.
