## The order-dependent failure my own test caught, and the pre-existing polluter behind it

    finally: news.GLOBAL_NEWS_ENGINE.get_news_calendar = orig

`get_news_calendar` is a **class** attribute, so `orig` is a bound method built on that access.

singleton and asserted `"get_news_calendar" not in engine.__dict__` → red.

1. **The polluter (real bug).** Converted both tests to `monkeypatch.setattr` — it records that the
