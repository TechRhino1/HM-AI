## Strategy diagnosis — negative net profit root cause
Wrote `docs/STRATEGY_DIAGNOSIS_AND_FIXES.md` (full report).

**Root cause identified:** exit-management defect, NOT entry quality.
- `be_trigger_r = 1.00` moves SL to entry after only 1R favourable movement.
- Structural SL too tight (`dynamic_sl_tp.py:53` uses `max()` = takes the CLOSER of struct/ATR stop).
- `runner_trail_atr = 2.2` is wider than the initial stop → ATR trail sits *behind* the stop and can never ratchet.
- Result: 99/206 trades exit at BE/TRAIL_SL averaging **+0.86R**, with **median MFE of 21.1R**. ~1,504R of favourable excursion abandoned.
- Only 10/206 trades (4.9%) ever reach TP.
- Baseline: 48.06% WR, PF 0.58, expectancy −$9.11, net −$1,876.56.

**Key quantified findings:**
- Moving `be_trigger_r` 1.0R → 2.0R alone flips expectancy from −0.005R to **+1.638R**.
- Combined entry filters + partial@0.5R exits → **69.3% WR, +0.908 avg R**.
- **Hard ceiling:** only 69.4% of trades reach +0.5R MFE, 80.1% reach +0.25R. So **75% WR is NOT achievable by exit changes alone** — needs entry-timing work too. Realistic path: 55% (P0) → 69% (P1) → 75-78% (P2 validated OOS).
- `master_score` has **zero discriminative power** (winners median 47 vs losers 48; reach-rate buckets non-monotonic). Gate rejects 4,504 XAUUSD setups while admitting median-score-47 trades.
- `BREAKOUT_EXPANSION`: 27.8% WR, −$459.36. `WEAK_TREND` regime: **14.3% WR**, −$397.50.
- OOS decay on FX: EURUSD 50.9%→38.5% (PF 0.50), USDCHF 57.1%→33.3% (PF 0.27), WFE 0.01–0.06 = overfit.
- Optimiser selects on tiny samples: USDJPY reports PF 99.0 / Sharpe 15.0 on **3 trades**.

**Prioritised fixes:** P0-1 raise be_trigger_r→2.0; P0-2 SL take wider level + min-distance floor; P0-3 trail_atr→1.2; P1-4 ban BREAKOUT_EXPANSION + block WEAK_TREND; P1-5 partial+runner exit structure; P1-6 refit/disable score gate; P1-7 min-sample gate in optimiser (≥30 trades, reject WFE<0.5); P2-8 per-symbol matrix (XAUUSD flagship keep, NAS100 increase, EURUSD/GBPUSD/SOLUSD/US30 disable).

### Fix 2 — learning loop correctness
- **R-multiple sign inversion** in `orchestrator._on_trade_closed`: formula keyed off win/loss, so SELL winners gave −5.0R and BUY losers gave +5.0R — poisoned the return-weighted SGD gradient. Now derives direction from `pending["type"]` / `decision.bias`; added `"type": decision.bias` to `_pending_features`.
- **Destructive drift protection** in `online_ml_predictor.update_online`: `weights *= 0.70` could fire on consecutive steps, compounding to ~50% signal loss, and reset `training_steps=10` (restoring large LR). Threshold 0.28 is barely above coin-flip 0.25. Now: threshold `0.28+0.07=0.35`, `brier_window_min=30`, `drift_cooldown_steps=100`, `drift_shrink_factor=0.92`. **40 consecutive losses now retain 78.4% of signal (was 30–50%), 1 drift event.**
- `online_ml_predictor.__init__` now wires `SETTINGS.ml` (was dead config) and resolves `model_file` to an absolute path via `_resolve_model_path` (live and backtest could otherwise load different models).
