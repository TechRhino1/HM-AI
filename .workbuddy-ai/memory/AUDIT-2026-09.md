## Headline: the entry signal has no measured edge

Measured on real MT5 bars, 20 symbols, SWING/H1 (and re-measured at 365d — see bottom).

* PF **0.568–1.202**; **1 of 40** symbol×target combos reaches the 1.3 acceptance bar. Win rate
  28.2–44.8% where a 1.5R target needs 40.0% to break even.
* At ~1% risk/trade, DD is 62–100%. DD ≤10% needs **0.012–0.108% risk per trade** — below the
  broker minimum lot on most symbols.
* Ruled out **by measurement**: costs (free execution still loses), exit geometry (best == seed),
  target width (PF invariant to tp), candidate ranking (no quantile breaks even), learned filter
  (AUC 0.481). What remains is the directional call.

### The directional call is 100% hand-authored

* 7-branch if/elif on `choch`/`bos`/`trend_score` — `decision_engine.py:105-122`.
* Gate "probability" = `0.45 ×` a hand-typed 6-bin table that *inflates* its inputs
  (`confidence.py:13-20`) `+ 0.55 ×` a hand-authored linear score clipped to [0.35, 0.88]
  (`online_ml_predictor.py:564-573, 417`).
* Verified 2026-09-16: **0% fitted**. `self.weights = DEFAULT_WEIGHTS.copy()` (literals),
  `bias = 0.20`, no `.fit()`, no persisted weights (`data/models/` holds only the meta-labeler).

### P0-1: do NOT wire up the "already-fitted" `ScoreCalibration`

The obvious fix is wrong. Measured on `config/winrate_profiles.json` (16 symbols):

* n is only **41–126 across 8 bins** (~5–16 trades/bin).

## Live-execution defects (acct 101059540)

* **Account is named "Demo Account"** in MT5 (`/api/diagnostics`) — LIVE mode, live logic, demo
  money. Confirm in-terminal before assuming real capital is at risk.
* **FIXED (`955d40c`) — `sl_price` and `risk_dist` disagreed by ~7×.** `dynamic_levels.py` floored
  `risk_dist` at `pip_size * 5` but never widened `sl_price` to match. The sizer prices risk off
  `sl_price` while the post-fill re-anchor (`execution_engine.py:95`) uses `sl_distance`, so a
  0.7-pip stop was sized at 0.69 lots and then re-anchored to 5 pips on an already-filled
  position: realised 4.6% against a 0.61% target. The floor now applies to the **distance**
  before `sl_price` is derived, and is dynamic — `max(3 × live spread, 10% ATR)`. Guarded by
  `test_sl_price_and_risk_dist_describe_the_same_level`.
* Both stops landed ~5 pips against a 2.0-pip spread — ~40% of the risk distance was spread
  cost. `execution_engine.py` still cannot resize after fill, so it now **warns** when the
  broker's minimum widens the stop past 1.25× the plan.
* **The sizer rejects the only symbols with edge.** WTI refused at `position_sizing.py:96-105`:
  "Minimum lot size (0.01) would force 3.29% risk (target 0.61%)". It then traded EURUSD +
  AUDUSD — the two worst-measured symbols. On a small account the lot grid, not the edge,
  decides what trades.
* `SR_RATCHET` fires every ~5s with 0.2-pip SL moves; MT5 answers "No changes" — no-op chatter.

### The broker-clock defect, in full (found and fixed 2026-09-16)

The backlog line said it "skews session logic". It is worse than that: it **disabled the
stagnation exits**.

MT5 reports `position.time`, `deal.time` and `symbol_info_tick().time` as seconds on the **broker
server's** clock. XM runs EET/EEST — GMT+2 in winter, GMT+3 in summer — so `mt5_client.py:240`
(`time.gmtime(p.time)`) and `database.py:201` (`datetime.fromtimestamp(t, timezone.utc)`) each put
the value 2-3 hours in the **future**.

`position_monitor._get_position_duration_sec` then computes `now_utc - open_dt`, which came out
**2-3 hours short**, and `max(0.0, ...)` flattened it to a hard **zero for the first three hours of
every position's life**. Because the horizon-adaptive block is gated on `if open_dur_sec > 0:`, the
whole time-decay section was skipped in exactly the window it exists to police:

* SCALP "45 minutes without progress" → could not fire until **3h45m**
* DAY_TRADING 6h stagnation → fired at **9h**
* SWING 36h compression → fired at **39h**
* `_determine_position_style` falls back to duration, so a **25-hour-old position read
  `DAY_TRADING` instead of `SWING`** — and style selects which exit rules and timeframe apply.

**Fix.** `jarvis/data/broker_time.py` derives the offset from the broker's own **freshest tick**
rather than hardcoding it (it moves with the broker's DST, differs per broker — +5:30 for India —
and deriving it survives a server change). Snapped to 30-minute granularity with a 300s tolerance
and a ±12/14h range check, so a **weekend-stale tick is rejected** instead of yielding a nonsense
offset. Cached 600s because each derivation costs an MT5 round-trip per symbol. On failure it
returns 0 — the previous behaviour — and **logs a warning once**, so the degradation is visible.
`mt5_client.get_open_positions` and `database.sync_mt5_history` now stamp true UTC.
The `max(0.0, ...)` clamp in `position_monitor` now **warns** instead of silently zeroing.

`tests/test_broker_time.py` — 13 tests. **Three of them fail on the pre-fix code**, including
`'DAY_TRADING' != 'SWING'` for the 25-hour position. Verified by temporarily restoring the buggy
line and re-running, not by assertion.
